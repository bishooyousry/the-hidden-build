# Facilitator guide: The Hidden Build · Executive Mission (about 15 minutes)

**Learning goal:** cascading goals. Share the full picture, not only details, and notice how personalities and styles change the way a goal is passed on.

**Timing:** intro 1 min · team-up 1-2 min · mission round 4 min · reveal and debrief 7-8 min.

## The story to tell (30 seconds)
"Each team has one mission: assemble a critical structure of the future organisation. One of you, the Messenger, sees the classified blueprint. Each of the others, the Builders, holds one part of the structure and never sees the whole. Work together so the parts join into one structure and bring it online."

## Setup
Share the link or the session code. Four teams (colours), one per breakout room if you use them. The first person to tap "I'm the Messenger" is the Messenger. No screenshots, no notes. Players can talk on a call; the in-game team chat is optional.

## The four structures (use them in the debrief)
| Structure | Leadership link | Cues a Messenger could use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy Tower | Strategy and vision | Stepped tower, orange power core on top, two glass windows, steel body widening to the base, energy-line foundation |
| Execution Bridge | Turning strategy into delivery | One tall steel pylon in the middle with an orange core on top, cyan cables fanning down to both sides, a white glass deck, a steel support below, energy-line water |
| KPI Dashboard | Measuring performance | Steel frame, a tall orange bar in the middle, cyan bars on either side at different heights, a white glass base |
| Team Network | Connection and trust | Orange nodes in the four corners, cyan energy lines running diagonally to a hub in the centre with steel above and below the core |

## Messenger pin: on or off?
The pin is **On by default**: the Messenger can pin the blueprint to their screen, so the challenge is communication rather than memory. Switch it **Off** in the console for a harder round: going back to remember makes the whole-picture question visible.

## Run
1. Press **START ROUND**. The mission countdown starts for every team at once. Do not hint that the whole picture matters.
2. Open the **Teams** cards and read the live chat if used. Notice: did the Messenger give the whole picture first or start with details? Was the wording clear or ambiguous? How did Builders react to each other?
3. Open the **Projector view**: the live city, team progress and, with **SHOW BUILDS**, each team's structure growing.
4. When teams finish (or **END ROUND NOW**), the structures are compared with the original. A perfect build comes **online**: it lights up, runs its own animation and reacts to touch. Press **Reveal leaderboard** for the staged ranking on the projector.

## Debrief (7-8 minutes)
- What are the guidelines for cascading a goal so everyone can act on it?
- Why did, or did not, the full picture come first? What changed once Builders knew what they were building?
- How did different personalities and styles shape how the Messenger described the structure and how Builders reacted?
- Ask a perfect team what made their parts fit together, and a team with a gap which part did not match and why.
- Link to leadership: what is the "strategy tower" of your own team, and who sees the blueprint?

The debrief panel shows accuracy per team, when the picture was named (a keyword check: read the chat too), blueprint views (peeks) and the clarity gap (the Messenger's self-rating against the Builders' average).

## Scoring
Accuracy = correct modules divided by (blueprint modules + stray modules), as a percentage. Team score = accuracy x 10 + a time bonus (0-100) that counts only if every Builder locked early and accuracy is at least 50%. Everyone on a team gets the team score.

## Tips
- If a Builder drops out mid-round the team waits for that lock or the timer. Use END ROUND NOW if needed.
- Music is Off by default (voices in breakout rooms).
- Free ntfy.sh may limit very large groups: test with two real devices first.
